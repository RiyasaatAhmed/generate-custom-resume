# Opportunity assessment and research

Archive the complete supplied posting in `job-post.md` with URL, retrieval date, title,
employer/recruiter, requisition ID, and posting status if known. Preserve the original
text rather than a generated reconstruction. If unavailable, request pasted text.
Do not identify an anonymous employer from speculation.

Before deep research, identify location restrictions, remote/hybrid expectations,
employment type, sponsorship/work authorization requirements, seniority, and required
application materials. Compare them with candidate-confirmed facts. Residence,
citizenship, authorization, and time-zone overlap are separate. Do not infer these
from names, nationality, previous clients, or a remote-work headline.

Use `evidence-map.json` to record direct/adjacent/missing/unknown evidence. Distinguish
hard restrictions from preferred qualifications and modest experience differences.
Return pursue, clarify first, or lower priority with specific reasons. Unknown facts
stay unknown; ask only questions that affect the recommendation. The user may still
choose a stretch role. Do not fabricate qualifications to accommodate that choice.

Research official careers, product/docs, engineering, changelog, and company pages.
Use recent primary sources for time-sensitive claims. External reporting may add context
with attribution. Candidate instructions can restrict sites or methods. Contact lookup
does not justify ignoring those restrictions.

Save `research.md` with:

```text
Employer/product/users:
Posting priorities and exact supporting passages:
Company observations: claim | URL | retrieved date | published date if available
Hiring-priority hypotheses: hypothesis | supporting observation | uncertainty
Unknowns/blocked sources:
Selected fit argument and supporting achievement IDs/source references:
Changes from base resume and why:
Open candidate questions:
```

A launch may suggest a relevant opportunity; it does not establish an unreported
performance problem. State hypotheses as hypotheses and avoid diagnosing their product
without evidence. Use one verifiable company detail when it helps the letter; do not
pad the research with values language that does not change the application.

Separate truthful keyword discoverability from employer-specific screening. ATS parsing,
keyword search, knockout questions, matching tools, and human review are different.
No universal score or title-weight claim is justified by local keyword counts.

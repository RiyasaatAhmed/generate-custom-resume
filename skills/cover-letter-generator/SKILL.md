# Cover letter strategy

Use [the shared cover-letter guide](../generate-custom-resume/references/cover-letter.md).
Select an opening supported by the input: a relevant achievement, an employer priority,
a verified company detail, or a genuine referral. Do not force a novel hook when a clear
introduction works. Explain the candidate's relevant judgment and why this employer.

Use the same truth and gap rules across all application materials. Do not create a fit
score or add invented measurements. A narrow letter request does not require research
beyond its needs, a complete resume rewrite, or application-state updates.

compatibility entrypoint shares the maintained guide instead of adding a second template.
